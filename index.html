Good questions

A little clarity
before we start.

How much does a website cost?

Website packages start at an indicative ฿35,000. Scope, content, integrations, and custom features affect the final price. Use the free estimator for a planning range, not a binding quote.

How long does a project take?

A typical business website takes 4–6 weeks after scope approval and receipt of content. E-commerce projects usually need 6–10 weeks. Your proposal confirms milestones and dependencies.

Can we work together outside Bangkok?

Absolutely. The studio model supports remote discovery, collaborative design reviews, and online handover for businesses across Thailand and beyond.

Will I be able to update my website?

A CMS can be included in your agreed scope. Training and a practical handover help your team manage everyday content without needing a developer.

What happens after the launch?

Support, hosting, maintenance, and response times should be agreed in your proposal. Ongoing care is available as a separately scoped service, not an assumed subscription.
